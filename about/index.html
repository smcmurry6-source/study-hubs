Part 1

The dashboard

The home page at smcmurry6-source.github.io/study-hubs. Bookmark this, not individual hubs.

Greeting and name

Says good morning/evening with your name. Everyone gets a random class name ("Gleaming Molar") until they pick one; it's what shows on every leaderboard.

Tap "Pick your name" / "Change name". "Go back to a random name" undoes it.

Your rank card

Your handpiece medallion, rank and level (e.g. Gold II), total XP, your place in the class ("#2 of 106"), a bar to the next level, and a Mastery row with your medals.

Timmy Tooth's house new

Your tooth buddy's home: his health bar, a status line, and buttons for a joke, a checkup and a secret tip. More in Part 6.

Next-exam hero

A giant countdown ("Today", "3 days") to the next exam, with Start studying and Daily drill buttons, and every other exam in the next 14 days underneath. On the right: your progress in that hub and your last two weeks of study days with your streak and weekly accuracy.

Send luck shared

The day before an exam and the morning of it (until 2 pm), a row lets you send luck once per exam. It shows how many classmates sent it and the latest names.

Hub cards

One card per live hub, grouped by class: what it covers, its modes, your progress on this device, a Daily drill row, "Updated" labels when the hub changes, and Open. A hub archives itself at 10 pm on its last exam day: its card greys out with no Open button and disappears 10 days later.

Exam check-ins

From 8 am on exam day a "Check in" button appears on the hub card; from 10 pm a pop-up asks once. Short questions: how ready you felt, how it went, how the hub compared to the exam, and which topics or question formats it missed. Topics and formats only: please don't write out exam questions or answers, since the honor code keeps exam content private. The answers shape the next hubs.

The class shared

Handpiece ranks (top 10 and the ladder of medallions), study streaks (days in a row), correct-in-a-row (longest live run and the all-time record), when people study (busiest hours) and arcade high scores for both hubs. Mastery medals show next to every name.

Studying now shared

The green pill in the top bar counts classmates with a hub open right now.

Hub recaps

After an exam, a shareable recap image of that hub (hours studied, accuracy, peak hour, the hardest question, awards) plays in a slideshow. Save image keeps it.

What's new

A timeline of every student-facing change, newest first.

Inbox

Replies to your reports and suggestions (kept 90 days) and notices to the whole class (60 days). The top-bar button shows an unread count; new items pop up once in the bottom right.

Settings and theme

The gear holds the same settings as a hub (below). The half-moon toggles light, dark or system theme.

Surveys

At most one short survey is live at a time and each person sees it once. Answering or "No thanks" both dismiss it.

Part 2

In every hub

A shared layer that every hub gets. On a computer it's the launcher in the bottom-right corner; on a phone it's the bottom bar (Search, Drill, Stats, More).

Search

Searches the full text of the hub (notes paragraphs, review rows, exam hints, cram lines and questions) and jumps to the exact spot. Magic words typed here also work (that's how phones do the easter eggs).

Daily drill

Ten questions a day per hub, fixed for the day: spaced-review questions that are due, questions you missed last time, then high-yield ones the class finds hard, at most 3 per lecture. Each card says why it was picked. Finish for a results screen, a "10 more" button and a drill streak.

The Drill tab (phone), the chip beside the launcher (computer), the drill buttons on the dashboard, or …/hubs/<hub>/#drill.

Spaced review

Every question you get right comes back after 1, then 2, 4 and 7 days, never later than the day before the exam. Misses come back sooner. It feeds the drill and Weak Spots.

Class stats

Your rank card, your mastery in this hub, your leaderboard flair, the trophy case, today's Golden Probe winner, Tooth Fairy collectors, the week's cavity finders, and Link my devices.

Class accuracy on every question

After you answer, the explanation shows what % of the class got it right. Explanations name the most popular wrong answer when it's a common trap.

Report an issue

A Report button on every question, plus one in the menu. When it's fixed you get a reply in your Inbox saying what changed.

Suggest something

Send an idea for the hubs. Replies come back the same way.

Listen

On Lecture Notes: pre-recorded narration of every lecture, at both reading levels, with seek, ±15 s, speed, resume where you left off, and lock-screen controls.

As taught / Plain English

Lecture Notes come in two reading levels: the professor's wording, or a plain-English rewrite. Each has its own narration.

Mind maps

"Show mind map" under each lecture draws its outline as a branching tree.

Settings

Font (Default, Serif, Sans) · Text size (Small to XX-Large) · Background sound (Soft rain, Ocean waves, Coffee shop, Lo-fi keys, Piano) · Accent (colors and metals you unlock by rank) · Surprises (all easter eggs on/off) · Timmy Tooth (Everywhere / Dashboard only / Off) · Nuke alerts (hide other people's strikes) · your display name.

Name badge

Your name (and rank numeral) sits in each hub's top bar with a pencil to change it.

Install as an app

"Add to Home Screen" gives a Gold-handpiece app icon and opens full screen. Pages load from cache when the connection is bad.

Part 3

Hub modes

Each hub has three modes in its top bar. Tabs vary slightly by hub; these are Perio's.

Compendium

Course Home (quick-start row, countdown, progress, course map, grade breakdown) · Lecture Notes (two reading levels, Listen, mind maps) · Question Bank (filter by lecture, type and source; exam-review sets; suture diagrams) · Mock Exam (exam-length, timed, exam format) · Weak Spots (your misses and what's due) · Active Recall (hide-and-reveal flashcards) · Cram Sheet · Exam Hints (the professors' own words).

Question types

Multiple choice, select-all with partial credit, matching, ordering, two-statement, EXCEPT / NOT, Patient Box cases and picture questions, each in the exam's own format.

Undo on ordering questions new

Tapped a step by mistake? Undo last step takes back your latest tap and Start over clears them all. Both show up once you've tapped a step and go away when the question is graded.

Review

Every reference table for the course in one place, searchable, with a "hide answers" switch to quiz yourself.

Arcade

Nine games per hub (next part). Games built on the question bank count toward your stats, XP and Timmy's health.

Part 5

Ranks, mastery and recognition

XP

10 XP for a question you get right for the first time, 2 for a repeat right answer, 1 for a miss (answer XP caps at 600 a day), plus 20 for every day you study. Counted across every hub.

Handpiece ranks

Seven handpiece medallions: Stone (start), Antique (300 XP), Bronze (1,500), Silver (5,000), Gold (12,000), Diamond (25,000), Dark Matter (50,000). Each has levels I, II and III. A new level gets a toast; a new rank gets a full celebration.

Accents

Each rank unlocks its metal as an accent in Settings, which paints buttons, switches and progress bars in that metal's texture.

Hub mastery

The share of a hub's question bank you got right on your latest try: Bronze 50%, Silver 75%, Gold 90%, Crown 100%.

Mastery medals new shared

Each mastery tier puts a medal (or a crown) next to your name on every leaderboard: rank, streak, correct-in-a-row, both arcades and Tooth Fairy collectors. Up to three show, best first, then "+N". Hover or long-press to see which hubs. A new medal gets its own pop-up.

Trophy case new: percentages

24 trophies in the Stats panel, 14 of them secret ("???" with a clue until you earn them). Each now shows the share of the class that has it ("61%", "<1%", "nobody yet"); tap one for the exact count. A "Psst" line under the grid gives a random clue for a secret you haven't found. Full list in Part 9.

Link my devices

Make a 6-character code on one device and enter it on another within 10 minutes; both then count as you (XP, streaks, trophies).

Part 6

Timmy Tooth new

An optional cartoon tooth buddy. Settings → Timmy Tooth: Everywhere, Dashboard only, or Off. Nobody else can see him or his health.

Meeting him

Before you meet him he sits on your name on the dashboard ("Psst. Up here. Tap me!"). Tapping opens a four-step introduction: who he is, how to take care of him, the fine print (he can't die; he can be turned off) and moving in. Finishing or closing it adopts him; "No thanks" turns him off. Adoption follows you to your other devices.

Where he lives

In a house card on the dashboard, and in the bottom-left corner of every hub with a health bar. He hides during mock exams.

Health

0 to 100 HP, worked out from your answers in every hub. Each right answer heals 0.8 HP (up to 30 a day), each day in a row adds a streak bonus (up to 10), and each skipped day costs 8, then 12, 16 and 20 HP the longer you're away. At 0 he can't die, but he heals at half speed until he's back to 50.

What goes wrong

Below 70 HP: plaque. Below 55: brown stains and red, puffy gums (gingivitis). Below 40: a cavity. Below 25: periodontitis (recession, calculus, loose). Below 12: a cracked cusp. At 0: a bandage. Each goes away as he heals, and he thanks you.

Talking to him

Tap him for one of 32 dental jokes (plus holiday and fall jokes in season); every few taps he gives a status update or a secret tip. Tap 5 times fast and he complains it tickles. "Checkup" opens his chart: health, today's answers, streak, every condition, and his house upgrades.

Secret tips new

He tells you how to find every easter egg, one at a time.

In order: the current holiday's word first (type fangs in October), then floss, mirror, Floss Chain, the Konami code, professor quotes, the Golden Probe, the Tooth Fairy, the Plaque Boss, Cavity Search, Full Arch, the root canal, Night Owl, the nuke, his own trophies, the trophy-case clues, and a stern warning never to type his name.

The "Secret tip" button on his house, every 4th tap, or sometimes as his hello in a hub.

Reactions

He greets you (and mentions it if you skipped yesterday), cheers at 3, 5, 10 and more right in a row, winces at misses and encourages you after a bad run, comments when you switch modes, reacts to every easter egg, naps if you go idle for 3 minutes, and gives pep talks the day before and the day of an exam.

His house

Gets an upgrade at each rank: picket fence (Antique), mailbox (Bronze), lamppost (Silver), gold trim and door (Gold), diamond windows (Diamond), a night sky (Dark Matter). A flagpole in the yard flies a pennant in the medal color of each hub you master, and any Crown mastery puts a crown on the roof. It also decorates for every holiday and season (Part 8).

His trophies

Pearly Whites for getting him to 100 HP; Full Recovery for bringing him from 0 back to 100.

Easter eggs

All of them switch off with Settings → Surprises and never appear during a mock exam. Magic words can be typed anywhere on a hub page (not in a text box), or into the hub's Search box on a phone.

These are the secrets. Want to find them yourself? Timmy's "Secret tip" button and the trophy case clues will nudge you. Otherwise, tick Show spoilers at the top.

floss Floss Boss

Three or more people type floss in the same hub within one minute.

Everyone in that hub gets a row of dancing teeth with the flossers' names, linked by floss.

mirror new Indirect Vision

Type mirror. Type it again to flip back.

The whole hub flips left to right, the way the maxillary arch looks in a mouth mirror.

TIMMY new

Type timmy on the dashboard or in any hub.

A 15-second show: the page goes dark and breaks apart, a crowned Timmy rises (his eyes follow your cursor; tap him to spin him), his name slams in letter by letter, mini Timmys rain down, and he blesses your study session before the page puts itself back together. Sound on recommended. Esc or the corner button ends it early. Not to be confused with Timmy Tooth, who has opinions about him.
