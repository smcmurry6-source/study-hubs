Gingival health and gingivitis

Deep dive. On an intact periodontium, health means probing depths of 3 mm or less and bleeding at fewer than 10% of sites.

In a successfully treated periodontitis patient, probing depths up to 4 mm still count as health if no 4-mm site bleeds.

Health versus gingivitis
PeriodontiumHealth
IntactProbing depths 3 mm or less, bleeding under 10%
Treated periodontitisProbing depths 4 mm or less, no 4-mm site bleeding, bleeding under 10%

Staging and grading

Stage is set first by interdental clinical attachment loss at the site of greatest loss.

  • Complexity factors such as deep probing depths or vertical defects can move a case up a stage.
