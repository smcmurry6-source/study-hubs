Design system

The type, color, drawings and question card every hub, the dashboard and the widget will be built from. Pick a typeface and look over each group of art; switch the hub to see its tint, emblem and scene.

Typeface
Theme

A hub: the bar, Home and a question

Timmy, your streak, the day's goal and your rank sit on the bar (at the bottom on a phone). Answer the question to see the rewards: the XP spark flies to your rank, Timmy reacts, and this answer closes the day's goal.

The question card, in every format

One card for every format. Each pair shows it waiting for an answer and answered: the verdict and explanation first, the class's picks beside each choice, and the why for your pick opened. Every card works: tap to answer.

Rewards

What moves when you answer: the XP spark, Timmy, the streak flame and the daily goal, and the recap after 25 answers or when you leave the bank.

Typeface

One face for the whole site, self-hosted. Here are both, side by side; the switch at the top sets the whole page in either one.

Tokens

One ground, one ink (Timmy's outline), one accent (reward gold) and a tint for each hub. Flat surfaces a tone step apart, one radius pair and the motion every control shares.

Icons

Drawn by hand on a 24 grid with one 2 px stroke and round ends, in the color of the text around them. For the bars, the notes toolbar, the card and More.

Hub emblems and masthead scenes

Each hub's emblem (on its tab, the dashboard and its medals) and the scene at the top of its Home, drawn in Timmy's ink, plus a pair for a future hub.

Mastery medals, trophies and ranks

The medal metal is the mastery level; the ribbons and the middle are the hub's. Every trophy is its own drawn object; a secret one shows as a shape with its riddle until it's won. The handpiece ranks stay as they are.

Timmy and his house, as drawn today

Rendered by the live code (widget/pet.js), unchanged: his moods, what shows as his HP drops, a few holiday outfits and the house as it grows with your rank.

Timmy's place

His house card, redrawn as a little world you build: pick his home, the scene around it and what stands in the yard. What you've earned (your rank, your pennants) and the season still show on any of them. Tap a spot to change it. This is a preview: there's no shop yet and nothing is saved.

The bowling egg

Eight short clips like the odd cartoons a bowling alley’s screens play after a strike, Timmy style: the pins are the Plaque crew and the ball is a giant sugar-free mint. A clip plays on the bar after a long run of first-try right answers, never over a question, and a tap skips it. This is a preview: nothing plays in the hubs yet.
