## Writing rules learned from T-01 (Claude had to fix these; please get them right first time)

1. **Write for students, not for the checker.** Never mention fact records, ids, "the record", "verified",
   "unverified", "quote-checked", "the source blocks scripts", or how a question would be "tested". When a source is
   weak, say so clinically ("a narrative review suggests", "this is expert opinion, not trial evidence"); when a
   number has no source, leave the number out rather than saying so.
2. **`stem_names` facts:** the sentence (or table row) that cites one must name one of its `stem_names` (for example
   "The ADA guideline...", "The AAPD..."). Acceptance check 6 lists any that don't.
3. **No 8 consecutive words from any quote** in the fact records (acceptance check 6 prints them). Fixed terms
   (drug names, a classification's official wording) are fine.
4. **Stay inside the fact's strength:** "relatively contraindicated" stays "relatively"; "may be considered" never
   becomes "give"; a pediatric (AAPD) number is never presented as the adult rule.
5. Archived or older sources are named as such ("the archived NIDCR guide").

## Blueprint (`blueprint.json`, same schema as `src/boards/units/medcx/blueprint.json`)

- 60 items, **27 in 6-8 cases** of 2-6 items (BUILD_SPEC 3.3). Weight toward analgesics and opioids, antibiotics and
  prophylaxis, interactions and drugs that change care.
- **Each section's `standalone + in_cases` must equal the sum of `siblings` over that section's concepts**, and a
  case item counts toward its concept's siblings (a concept used twice in cases needs `siblings` of at least 2). This
  was wrong in 10 of 14 medcx sections.
- About 35-45 concepts; 8-12 marked `hy` with `siblings` 2-3; `fk` from FK8 only (U15), `cc` from CC25, CC26, CC27.
- Case boxes: realistic, everything the items need is in the box, nothing that hands over an answer. Adult patients
  for adult rules; if a case needs AAPD numbers, make the patient a child or teen. Avoid values where the sources
  contradict themselves (the fact's `conditions` say where).
- Cases may include a medcx or perio item (`"units": ["pharm", "medcx"]`) when the patient naturally needs it.
