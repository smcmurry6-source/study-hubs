About this hub

Hepatobiliary Hub is four ways into the same material: Compendium for reading, reference, and exam-day tools like the Mock Exam and Readiness Dashboard; Atlas for the anatomy and histology you have to see to know; On Call for drilling it under a little pressure; and Quest for the same drilling with a coin purse and a tyrant to defeat. All four read from one shared bank of lectures, questions, drugs, hormones, and diagrams — flag a question as a Follow-up in the Compendium and it shows up as a Follow-up in On Call too, because it's the same underlying record, not separate copies.

Coverage right now

Built from Exam 2's practice materials (the Quizzes, Crossword, and Slido review) plus the eight lecture decks listed in the Compendium's Lectures tab. Class hasn't happened yet for this exam, so there's no lecture recording or learning-objectives sheet to draw "likely to be tested" hints from the way a hindsight study guide could — the Exam Signal tab is built honestly from what's actually in the practice material instead of guessing at emphasis.

Adding new material later

When more lecture slides, a recording, or an objectives sheet get uploaded, the new content gets folded into the shared data (the lecture list, the question bank, the drug/hormone tables, the atlas diagrams) — not rebuilt from scratch. Every mode reads live from that same data, so one update surfaces everywhere: a newly-loaded lecture's questions appear in the Quiz Bank, its ward unlocks in On Call, and its coverage chip flips from pending to loaded.
