# Figures teach the lecture: outside sources for accuracy, the lecture for content

Sam's rule, 2026-11-04, for every figure from now on (anima figures, drawn `FIGS`, case images, diagram questions):
**outside sources are welcome to make a figure more accurate, but what it shows and names follows what the lecture
teaches and emphasizes**, so it stays high yield. For example, an anima of a hormone signaling pathway draws the steps,
receptors and drugs the lecture covers, not every enzyme and messenger a textbook adds.

- **Use outside sources**: since 2026-11-06 they are encouraged on every figure, for accuracy and for how the figure
  works, and cited where the figure's sources go ([outside-sources-for-figures](outside-sources-for-figures.md)).
- **Keep the content the lecture's**: the parts you label, number, key, hover-describe or mention in a caption are the
  ones the lecture (the hub's notes, its hints and review guides) teaches. Use the lecture's own words and numbers.
- **Leave out what the lecture doesn't teach**, even when it's true and the source has it: extra enzymes, intermediate
  steps, part names, measurements, eponyms. Draw a real part unlabeled if the picture needs it to look right; don't name
  it. If a number from a source sets the scale (a layer drawn to its true thickness), the drawing can use it while the
  labels show only the lecture's numbers.
- **Where the source and the lecture disagree**, follow the lecture and tell Sam (in the PR), as for any content
  question; don't quietly draw the source's version.
- What the class struggles with (`question_stats`, reports) can decide which taught points a figure brings forward.

How it's done: [diagrams-anima](diagrams-anima.md) (the house rules for drawn figures) and
[diagram-review](diagram-review.md) (which figures get built). First applied to the PCD Implants figures: the seven
implant types draw true shapes from Linkow's texts and the patents, but their keys name only what L1 teaches, and the
tooth vs implant figure is drawn to Gargiulo's and Cochran's layers while its labels give only the lecture's 2.04 and
3.08 mm.
