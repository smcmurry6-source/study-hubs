# Outside sources are encouraged for every figure

Sam's standing rule, 2026-11-06, for every figure from now on, in every hub (anima figures, drawn `FIGS`, case images,
diagram questions, games that draw anatomy or a mechanism): **outside sources are welcome and encouraged, to make a
figure more accurate and to make it work better.** Don't stop at the hub's notes when a good source can make the drawing
truer or its interaction more faithful.

- **Look for them on every figure.** They're part of building it, not an extra. Use atlases, textbooks, review articles,
  classic studies, guidelines, and manufacturers' specifications and patents.
- **For accuracy:** true shapes, proportions, positions and scale, how a structure or device really looks, and real
  values (how far, how fast, how much) where a figure would otherwise guess.
- **For functionality:** how the mechanism really behaves, so the interaction is honest. That covers what moves, in what
  order, how far, what drives it (the mechanics, physiology or pharmacology) and what each control should change. Good
  ways other explainers make the same idea interactive count too.
- **Cite them** where the figure's sources go: anima's "What this shows, and what's simplified", or the build file's
  header. Say what each source settled. Prefer primary or authoritative sources. Anything still a guess is called
  schematic or illustrative there.
- **What a figure names and tests still follows the lecture**
  ([figures-teach-the-lecture](figures-teach-the-lecture.md)). Sources make the drawing and its behavior right; the
  lecture decides what is labeled, keyed and asked about, and wins when the two disagree (tell Sam in the PR).
- **Draw it anew from what a source says.** Never copy a source's artwork, and never use another class's course material
  or graded work ([graded-coursework](graded-coursework.md)).
