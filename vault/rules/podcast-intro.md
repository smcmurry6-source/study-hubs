# Timmy Time's cold open skips the minutiae

Sam's rule, 2026-11-04, for every episode from now on: the opening says which exam it is, the day and which lectures it
covers, then gets to the material. **Leave out** how many questions come from each lecture, the exact time of the exam
or its window, LockDown Browser and other logistics (laptop charging, quiz due dates). The sign-off says the day, not
the time. The exam card on the hub's Home page already carries those facts (`examCardHTML`).

What still belongs in the open: Timmy's greeting and a lore beat, the exam and its lectures, the professors and how the
episode is ordered. A fact about the exam that changes how to study (for example, that some questions ask for every
right answer) may go where it helps, briefly.
